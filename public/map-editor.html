Drawing tools help

Click or drag to paint or erase blocks. Pillars, barriers and cars place groups of wall or cover blocks. Each click adds a new numbered player or enemy. Objects cannot overlap. Targets mark a cell; gates are drawn along the outside border. Select Gate, then click or drag along the border to set its length.
